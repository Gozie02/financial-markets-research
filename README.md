# Selected Financial Markets Research

A compact sample of independent research on how macroeconomic and market developments transmit through rates, FX, financing conditions, corporate cash flows and asset prices.

**[Read the selected research sample (PDF)](Gozie_Ibekwe_Selected_Financial_Markets_Research.pdf)**

The sample brings together three investigations that began with a live market question, followed the transmission mechanism, and changed direction when the evidence did not support the original hypothesis.

## Research included

### ReNew Energy Global — financing, rates and hedging
Rising bond yields made financing-sensitive renewable developers worth another look. I used ReNew as a case study because its disclosures allowed me to examine fixed and floating-rate debt, currency exposure, maturities, benchmark-linked borrowing and hedging, then test how much rate sensitivity remained after those protections.

### African currency markets — forecast risk under regime change
This work began with a simple hypothesis about stale African FX series and zero-shot forecasting. The first result did not support that hypothesis. The accepted short paper at **GlobalSouthAI @ NeurIPS 2026 (Affinity Event)** developed from that investigation, and the separately versioned post-acceptance work extended the question to regime changes and probabilistic calibration.

### Rhine 2026 low-water shock — from physical exposure to financial sensitivity
The original hypothesis was that companies with heavier Rhine exposure should suffer more as water levels and freight capacity deteriorated. Operational evidence confirmed the physical shock, but the equity signal was not clean. The investigation instead pointed to **logistics redundancy, inventory buffers and pricing power** as the more useful state variables.

## How I approach markets

> Catalyst → expectations → transmission mechanism → exposure → instrument / expression → risk and invalidation → update.

The aim is to understand how a market story reaches prices, cash flows or balance sheets — and to treat a rejected trade or hypothesis as a useful research outcome when the evidence does not support it.
